Privacy Policy — WorkstationHub Studio

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

WorkstationHub Studio is a local desktop creator utility operated exclusively on the channel owner's own workstation.

1. Google API Services & Data Access

WorkstationHub Studio accesses YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API solely to upload videos, inspect channel details, and manage playlists authorized directly by the signed-in channel owner via Google OAuth 2.0.

2. Local-Only Storage & Zero Third-Party Sharing

No user data, credentials, or OAuth tokens are transmitted to or stored on any external third-party server. Credentials and OAuth tokens are saved strictly locally on the user's own computer.

3. Revoking Access

You may revoke WorkstationHub Studio's access at any time via Google Account Permissions and delete local token files on your workstation.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
